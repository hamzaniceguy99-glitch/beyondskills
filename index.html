How we work

Not a recruiter, and no leaked questions

Beyond Skills has no relationship with any employer. We submit nobody, refer nobody and take no placement fee, which means the advice is about your performance rather than about filling a role.

We also won't supply any company's confidential interview questions, and we'd suggest avoiding anyone who does — it's a good way to be disqualified. Practice on public problem sets and general patterns instead; that's what actually transfers.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do you get me interviews?

No. We're not a recruiter and take no placement fee. We coach performance; applications stay with you.

I can solve problems but freeze in interviews.

Extremely common, and exactly what mocks fix. The skill is performing while being watched, and it's separate from the engineering.

Which language should I use?

Whichever you're fastest in. Interviewers care about reasoning far more than syntax, with rare exceptions we'll flag.

Do you have the real questions from company X?

No, and we wouldn't use them. Confidential materials can get you disqualified. Public patterns prepare you perfectly well.

I'm senior — is this still useful?

Senior loops lean on system design and behavioral depth, which is where many strong engineers underperform. Those programs are built for exactly that.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.
