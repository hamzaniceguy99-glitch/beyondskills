The programs, in detail

Four programs of 8 to 12 weeks, built around timed mock interviews every week.

Program 01 · Out loud, under time

Coding rounds

The code matters less than the process around it. We work on clarifying the question before starting, narrating your reasoning, choosing a workable approach rather than the perfect one, stating complexity honestly, and recovering out loud when you hit a wall — which interviewers specifically watch for.

  • 10 weeks
  • Can code already
  • 8 hours a week
  • Any language

Program 02 · Reasoning, not memorizing

System design

Design rounds reward structured thinking over memorized architectures. We work on gathering requirements, making rough estimates out loud, sketching components, and discussing trade-offs honestly — including admitting what you don't know, which lands far better than bluffing.

  • 12 weeks
  • Mid-level and up
  • 8 hours a week
  • A whiteboard tool

Start this program

Program 03 · Stories with substance

Behavioral rounds

Behavioral rounds sink more candidates than coding does. We build a set of real stories from your experience, structure them so the outcome is clear, and rehearse the follow-up questions — especially the conflict and failure ones, where vague answers are obvious.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any level
  • 4 hours a week
  • Your work history

Program 04 · The last mile

Process & offers

The end of a search has its own problems: overlapping timelines, take-homes that eat weekends, and an offer you have four days to answer. We plan the process, scope take-homes sensibly, and prepare the compensation conversation — though contracts and legal questions go to an attorney.

  • 6 weeks
  • Interviewing now
  • 4 hours a week
